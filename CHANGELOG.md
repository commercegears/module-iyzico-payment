# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-06)
- First release: iyzico Checkout Form payments by redirect or in a frame on your page (Luma and Hyvä).
- Payments verified with iyzico before capture; declined and abandoned orders cancelled, basket restored.
- Fraud reviews, lost callbacks and duplicate payments handled automatically.
- Online full and partial refunds from credit memos, and a transactions grid with CSV export.
- Optional installment table on product pages, REST endpoints for headless stores, English and Turkish.
